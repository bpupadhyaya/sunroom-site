Blink

A calm picture flickers between two versions. One small thing has changed. Can you see it?

Coming soon to theApp Store & Google Play

How it works

Watch the picture blink between the original and an altered copy. Tap the spot where something moved, vanished, shifted or changed colour. Take your time: there is no countdown.

Ways to play

  • Calm: 240 levels that ease in gently.
  • Daily: one new picture a day, the same for everyone, with a streak and a shareable postcard.
  • Zoom: closer, finer changes, up to five times magnification.
  • Gallery: collect 38 pictures of cosy rooms and quiet places.
Free to play. Ads are optional to watch for hints, and one purchase removes them for good.
