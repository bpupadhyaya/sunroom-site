Spot the quiet change

Blink

One small thing in a calm picture has changed. Look at your own pace, then tap it.

Coming soon toiPhone and AndroidAll games

How it works

The picture never flashes on its own. You choose how to look: drag a soft lens over the picture, scrub a curtain between the two versions, close soft eyelids once and look again, or pick the one altered copy out of four. Then tap what changed. There is no countdown.

Ways to play

Four ways to lookLens, curtain, eyelids and odd one out. Pick your favourite.
DailyOne new picture a day, the same for everyone, with a streak and a shareable postcard.
ZoomFiner changes, up to five times magnification.
GalleryCollect 106 pictures of rooms, kitchens, gardens, houses and views.
Free to play. Ads are optional to watch for hints, and one purchase removes them for good.
