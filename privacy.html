Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026. This policy covers the Sunroom games (Blink, Patchtown, Plotwise and Mistwalk), published by EqualInformation LLC.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Your progress, settings and streaks are stored on your device only.

Ads

The games are free and show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and other device information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s own policies. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) you are asked for consent first and can change your choice in the game’s settings. You can also limit ad personalisation in your device settings.

Purchases

An optional “remove ads” purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation that the purchase was made, not your payment details.

Children

The games are for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13.

Sharing pictures

If you share a postcard or result, it goes through your device’s share sheet to the app you choose. We do not receive it.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@equalinformation.com.
