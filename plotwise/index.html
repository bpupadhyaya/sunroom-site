Count, plant, harvest

Plotwise

Plant orchards on a little farm. Numbers on every field, row and column tell you how many trees belong there.

Coming soon toiPhone and AndroidAll games

How it works

Each coloured field shows how many trees it holds, and tags along the edges give the count for every row and column. Trees never share a side, ponds can never hold a tree, and a few trees come pre-planted. Every plot has exactly one answer you can reach by thinking, never by guessing.

Ways to play

Journey300 plots that grow from tiny to tricky.
DailyOne new plot a day, the same for everyone.
RelaxNo clock, endless plots.
FarmEvery plot you finish adds to your farm map.
