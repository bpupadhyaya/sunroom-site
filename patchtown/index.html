Patchtown

Slice, mix and rebuild. Put the pieces of a house back together, or mix them into something new.

Coming soon to theApp Store & Google Play

How it works

Each house or room arrives as pieces: setting, roof, walls, entry and garden. Drag them into place. Rebuilding the original earns the top score; mixing pieces from different houses scores by how well they fit in style, colour, light and seams. Pip the gecko cheers you on.

Ways to play

  • Quick Part for a few minutes, Whole House and Room for a full build.
  • House + Setting adds the garden and surroundings.
  • Mashup mixes pieces from different homes.
  • Street lets you line up four lots and keep them in a street gallery.
  • Daily Box gives everyone the same crates each day.
Free to play. Ads are optional to watch for help, and one purchase removes them for good.
