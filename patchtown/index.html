How it works

Each house or room arrives as pieces: setting, roof, walls, entry and garden. Drag them into place. Rebuilding the original earns the top score; mixing pieces from different houses scores by how well they fit in style, colour, light and seams. Pip the gecko cheers you on.

Ways to play

Quick PartA few minutes with one piece of a home.
Whole House & RoomBuild a full house or a single room.
MashupMix pieces from different homes.
Street & Daily BoxLine up four lots, and get the same daily crates as everyone.
Free to play. Ads are optional to watch for help, and one purchase removes them for good.
