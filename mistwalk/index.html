How it works

Paper lanterns sit on the board. Each number is how many cells that lantern can see along its row and column, counting its own cell, until the edge or the first mist bank. Lift the mist from the cells you are sure about and leave banks where you are not. Banks never touch by side, and the cleared cells form one connected walk. Every level has one answer you can reach without guessing.
